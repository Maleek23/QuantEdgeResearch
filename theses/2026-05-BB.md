QNX is the under-the-hood layer that doesn't get press but is *spec'd in* on most premium ICE and EV programs because of ISO 26262 / functional-safety certification. Switching cost is brutal — re-certifying a stack is a multi-year program. That's the moat.

**Analog from prior cycles:**
- $LITE was the photonics moat under the AI optical buildout. The market priced it as a transceiver vendor; it was actually the indium-phosphide bottleneck. By the time consensus updated, the multiple had already expanded.
- $BB is the automotive moat under the SDV/AI-cockpit buildout. The market prices it as a former-phone meme. It's actually the certified RTOS bottleneck.

That's the bet. Same shape of trade, different sector, different cycle phase.

**Why $BB and not $NOK:** $NOK's narrative is networking infra capex — that's a commodity bid against Ericsson and Huawei in a multi-year capex cycle, with margin compression risk. $BB's narrative is **software royalty re-rating** — same revenue base can support 2–4x the multiple if the market re-tags the segment. Asymmetric upside vs. $NOK's flat-to-modest grind.

## 7. Risks & invalidation

**Thesis-breaking (these are the things that make me wrong):**
1. QNX revenue *decelerates* on a clean comp — the whole re-rate depends on growth holding. A flat-to-down QNX print at constant FX is the primary kill.
2. OEMs in-house an open-source RTOS at scale (e.g. Linux + safety patches reach functional parity). This is a multi-year risk, not a quarter risk, but watch for it.
3. Margin structure stays at consolidated-blend levels — meaning the QNX leg *isn't* high-margin software in disguise. SOTP framework collapses.

**Execution risk:**
- Continued share dilution / SBC creep
- Capital allocation misstep (overpaying for an acquisition)
- Loss of certification / safety incident on a flagship program

**Macro/sector:**
- Auto cycle rolls over (high rates, EV demand wobble) → near-term royalty growth slows
- Risk-off in small-mid cap software → multiple compression independent of fundamentals

**Hard invalidation:**
- Weekly close below `[VERIFY support-2 level]` → cut core, keep small LEAPS only
- One full quarter of QNX YoY decline at constant FX → exit, regardless of price action
