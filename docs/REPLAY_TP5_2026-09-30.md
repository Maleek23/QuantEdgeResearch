## Why this exists

Operator ask: "any NEXUS play that triggered and then ran 5–10% should count as a win (e.g. GOOGL now)".
Counting a trade as a win because price later passed a level it never exited at is hindsight
max-favourable-excursion, so the win definition stays as it is. Two honest alternatives shipped instead:

1. **Run-up metric** (live, `shared/run-up.ts`): for every triggered idea since 2026-08-26, the best
   underlying move after the trigger and whether +3/+5/+10% came *before the stop*. Shown next to the
   strict win rate as "Reached +5% after trigger, before stop — not the win rate".
2. **This replay:** what the record would have been if the plan had actually *taken* profit at +5%.

## Pre-registered variants (fixed before any data was read)

- **actual** — plan as published: full exit at T1, stop, or horizon close (replayed on the same bars)
- **A** — full exit at +5% on the underlying (T1 replaced), stop unchanged, else horizon close
- **B** — full exit at the nearer of +5% or 1R (T1 replaced), stop unchanged, else horizon close
- **C** — half at +5%, stop on the rest moved to breakeven; the rest exits at T1, breakeven, or horizon
  close (before +5% is reached: plan as published)

Rules: regular-session bars (crypto 24h), 5-minute where Yahoo has them (≤57 days back), else hourly.
The path starts at the trigger (`executionAudit.triggerObservedAt` → `executionRecordedAt` → first bar
through the entry after publish). Stop fills at the stop or a worse gap open; take-profits fill at the
level; stop and take-profit in one bar = stop. R = underlying move ÷ |entry − stop|. Horizon: `exit_by`
when stored, else `horizonTradingDays` sessions. Ideas with an incomplete horizon are "open" and left
out of the stats. Options ideas are evaluated on the underlying; option P&L differs (theta, IV, delta).
Walk-forward: split at the calendar midpoint of trigger times; a variant counts as better only if it
beats "actual" on expectancy in both halves.

Reported per variant, overall and per half: n, win rate, avg R, avg %, expectancy R, profit factor —
next to the recorded outcome (classifyOutcomeV2 / realisedR) for the same ideas.
